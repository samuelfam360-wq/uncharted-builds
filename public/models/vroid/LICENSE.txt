body-male.vrm, body-female.vrm, hair-standard.vrm, hair-long.vrm: made in code by
Samuel Fam's Maker Studio character maker (samuelfam360-wq/maker-studio, src/character).
Original work for Uncharted Builds. The hair files are whole characters on the male body;
the game takes only their hair (and its swinging-hair settings) and puts it on the hero's body.

sample.vrm: "VRM1_Constraint_Twist_Sample" by pixiv Inc. ((c) 2022 pixiv Inc.),
from the pixiv/three-vrm samples. Slimmed for the web (smaller textures, flat normal maps).
Its VRM 1.0 licence (https://vrm.dev/licenses/1.0/, stored in the file's meta) says:
avatar use by everyone, commercial use including corporations, violent content allowed,
modification and redistribution allowed, credit not required.
It now only stands in if the Maker Studio files fail to load.
